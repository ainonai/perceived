# Artwork and photography

The site combines 16 real photographs with three generated editorial images. All assets are stored locally, and credits appear on the page and in `assets/photo-credits.json`. The small Caesar channel avatar was supplied by the project owner and is used only beside the Julius profile.

## Generated images in edition 03

| Asset | Native size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/artwork/marble-bust-crimson.webp` | 1122 × 1402 | Hero portrait |
| `assets/artwork/marble-reflections.webp` | 1774 × 887 | Layered marble-and-mirror scene |
| `assets/artwork/night-road.webp` | 1672 × 941 | Very subtle decorative forest background |

The images are editorial illustrations, not documentary photographs or authenticated museum objects. The earlier edition's marble-bust asset is retained as a previous design asset.

The portrait brief specified ivory Roman marble, a near-black background, sculpted detail, and vivid crimson rim light. The reflection brief specified two marble busts, a dark mirror, and red architectural light. The forest brief specified a nocturnal road, cold mist, dark tree silhouettes, and small distant headlights. Generated outputs were converted to WebP without enlarging the full-size originals.

Layered images and lighting give the portrait and reflection scenes their depth. The forest artwork is fixed behind the reading page.

## Real photographs

Fourteen photographs come from Unsplash and two from the NASA/ESA/CSA Webb image collection. Subjects include forests, a foggy night road, city life, relationships, mountains, a library, a microscope, an ocean, chess, a staircase, peacock plumage, newspapers, a smartphone, the Pillars of Creation, and the Cosmic Cliffs. The page records the photographer or agency, source page, use guidance, and image changes for each.

The edition 03 night photographs are credited to [Filippo Molinari](https://unsplash.com/photos/mysterious-foggy-forest-with-dark-bare-trees-bz1SwQlCWYk) and [myung lee](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-foggy-street-at-night-with-street-lights-nxN62qCJOnY). The real foggy-road photograph is used for the large reading interlude and the society chapter. It is separate from the generated decorative background.

Stock photographs and artwork illustrate topics and atmosphere; they do not establish adjacent scientific or social claims. Pictured people are not identified as participants in the transcript narratives. Keep the credits when republishing.

## Sharing card

`assets/social-card.svg` is native vector artwork for link previews, rendered to the 1200 × 630 `assets/social-card.png`. It uses the Perceived eye/P emblem, the Cormorant Garamond wordmark as vector outlines, plain informational typography, and a red gradient. It is separate from the generated editorial illustrations and does not add another AI-generated image.

## Final reading pass

The universe cover uses the wide Cosmic Cliffs observation at 3840 pixels across, avoiding a narrow portrait crop stretched across the chapter. New newspaper and smartphone photographs illustrate the information and social-media subsections. Nine subsection figures include topic-specific captions; the chess photograph now fills the reading column. Corrupt mountain, ocean, and Pillars thumbnails were regenerated from the valid local originals.

The archive handoff removes the fog, pointer and scroll effects, and keeps the fixed forest artwork. The earlier reading pass removed the sound panel and audio code at the owner’s request.
