## 2. Choose the outcome before choosing the tactic

Julius prioritizes being genuinely desired. A relationship acquired primarily through provision is often a lesser outcome in his personal ranking, although he allows that other men may prefer it. He also wants children with a woman whose values he respects, ideally in a monogamous family. At other times he praises multiple simultaneous relationships and persistent attachment after separation. ## 4. Meeting people: reciprocity, approaches, and incidental interaction

Julius recommends responding to indications of interest, such as a smile or sustained eye contact. He says unsolicited cold approach signals low options and will usually fail; his strongest version says it cannot work. As a workaround, he suggests creating an apparently incidental reason to talk that gives a woman another chance to select him. He acknowledges shyness, subtle signals, and exceptions. ## 15. A practical decision sequence with the provenance kept visible

Improve appearance where it makes a meaningful difference, recognize an opening with someone who shows interest, and build a life with real priorities. Supply enough care to sustain the relationship you want. Near your own desirability level, the combination of alpha and beta qualities becomes especially important. **Congruence** connects a strategy to the person using it. A predominantly provider strategy and an alpha-leaning strategy begin from different strengths.
