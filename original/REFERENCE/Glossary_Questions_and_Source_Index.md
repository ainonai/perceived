## Questions a reader is likely to ask

### Q01. Is the atlas endorsing “laws of female nature”?

No. It reconstructs proposed principles and labels evaluation separately. A recurrent assertion across videos establishes its importance to Julius, not its truth. The science review tests narrower propositions and identifies unverified ones.

### Q02. Does he think only looks matter?

His early language often approaches that claim. Later he expressly recognizes logistical attraction, care, personality enjoyment, purpose, and relative discrepancy. The most faithful answer is “looks dominate his theory, but he does not consistently exclude other influences.” [V003 · 2022-06-05 · 11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E7rekEt5whE&t=666s) [V190 · 2026-08-23 · 01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jq6SD2UkJFs&t=101s) [V198 · 2026-10-17 · 09:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUDSQGjoE0I&t=554s)

### Q03. What is the difference between desire and retention?

He allows someone to desire a man yet leave because the arrangement is insecure or unworkable, or remain for provision without intense desire. These are distinct outcomes, especially in V083, V127, and V190. [V083 · 2026-04-10 · 00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G2YXmz68BHM&t=0s) [V127 · 2026-05-19 · 29:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C9jRj0Lx69I&t=1753s) [V190 · 2026-08-23 · 03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jq6SD2UkJFs&t=218s)

### Q04. Does “90% looks” come from a measured model?

No fitted model or dataset establishing that percentage is supplied. It functions as a recurring heuristic. The outcome and denominator change across discussions, so it cannot be treated as a scientific coefficient.
